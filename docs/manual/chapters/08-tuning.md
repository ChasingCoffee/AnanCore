## Tuning, VFOs, bands, and split

- **VFO display:** full-size numerals; scroll a digit to step that decade, click the digits to type a frequency (kHz) inline. The G2 layout puts the same live readout on each receiver's flag.
- **Two receivers, VFO A/B:** RX1/VFO-A and RX2/VFO-B each have their own frequency, mode, filter, and levels. Choose which VFO transmits; **SPLIT** transmits on B while receiving on A (a red SPLIT▸B tag lights on RX1's flag).
- **RIT:** receive incremental tuning rides its own control (front-panel encoder aware); **LOCK** freezes the VFO against accidental spins.
- **XIT:** transmit incremental tuning offsets the *transmitted* carrier by up to ±99 999 Hz without moving the dial — the receiver stays where it is. Toggle it with the **XIT** key; while it is on, a chip beside it steps the offset in 10 Hz (scroll, or −/+; middle-click zeroes). Whenever XIT is on — including when it was switched on from the G2 front panel, a logging program over CAT or TCI, or a MIDI controller — RX1's VFO flag shows a red **XIT ±n** tag, so an offset transmitter is never invisible. On the G2 front panel, the **RIT/XIT** button cycles off → RIT → XIT → off, **CLEAR** zeroes both, and the RIT encoder adjusts XIT while XIT is the active one.
- **Tuning step:** the STEP control cycles preset steps; the step also feeds the front panel's dial behaviour (§18).
- **Bands and band memories:** band buttons remember per-band frequency, mode, and filter automatically — tap 20m and you're back where you left 20m. Per-band PA gain and OC (open-collector) outputs follow the band too.
- **Frequency calibration:** Settings → CALIBRATION, one button against WWV 10 MHz (§21).
